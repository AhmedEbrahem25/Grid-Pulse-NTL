02

How we make money — three streams

Lead with a hardware sale + a light SaaS fee, then capture upside on the energy we actually recover. Utilities can instead take Hardware-as-a-Service — we finance the node for a higher recurring fee.

STREAM 01 · one-time

Hardware

EGP 6,000 / node

Edge node + share of gateway, sold at ~25% integration margin. Or leased as HaaS to shift CapEx off the utility's books.

STREAM 02 · recurring

SaaS platform

EGP 300 / node / month

Detection engine, live map, explainable verdicts, inspection workflow, loss-analytics & reporting. ~75% gross margin.

STREAM 03 · performance

Recovery share

15% of recovered energy

A cut of verified recovered billing for the first 24 months per zone. Aligns us with the utility — we win only when they do.

Unit economics — one node

A single instrumented transformer, over a five-year life. The hardware is a one-time margin; the recurring SaaS and recovery share are where the value compounds.

The customer's side of the math

EGP 3,500 / mo

recovered when a node catches one continuous ~3 kW illegal draw (≈2,160 kWh/month at recovered tariff).

Against an EGP 6,000 node, that is payback in under two months — and most theft is larger. This is why the sale closes.

06

Go-to-market — land, prove, mandate, scale

In a B2G market you win with a measured pilot and a recovery number the ministry can defend. Then the framework tender does the scaling for you.

PHASE 01

Land a paid pilot

M0–M6

One high-NTL district, 500 nodes. Publish an audited recovery figure with the EDC.

PHASE 02

Prove the number

M6–M14

Independent verification of recovered energy; case study with the Ministry. Turn it into a reference.

PHASE 03

Win the mandate

M14–M24

Framework tender across EDCs; regulator ties deployment to loss-reduction targets.

PHASE 04

Scale & expand

Y3+

National rollout, then MENA utilities with the same NTL problem. Data moat compounds.

07

The ask & use of funds

Seed capital to fund the pilot, harden the hardware, and reach the Year-3 EBITDA turn. Hardware CapEx at scale is met with asset financing, not equity.

EGP 30M

≈ $625K seed — carries the company through the district pilot and two years to the profitability turn.

Hardware for large deployments (Years 3–5) is funded through asset-backed financing / leasing against signed EDC contracts, keeping the equity raise small relative to the EGP 600M+ revenue it unlocks.
