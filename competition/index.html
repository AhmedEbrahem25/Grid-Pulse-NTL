01

Six ways utilities fight theft today

Each has a real strength — and a structural gap that leaves theft hiding inside natural loss.

Boots on the ground

Manual field audits

in-house crews · tip lines
+Ground truth — an inspector confirms the hook.
–Random, tip-driven, unrepeatable; labour-heavy and slow.
Meter everyone

Full AMI / smart meters

Landis+Gyr · Itron · Honeywell · Sensus
+Per-customer billing data; the long-term gold standard.
–EGP 1,500–3,000 × every customer; years to roll out; the meter itself can be bypassed.
Analytics on meter data

AMI / MDM NTL analytics

Bidgely · Awesense · Siemens EnergyIP · Oracle · C3 AI
+Powerful ML on rich per-meter data.
–Needs the AMI rollout to exist first — no smart meters, no input.
Grid sensing

LV grid / feeder monitoring

DEPsys GridEye · Sensus · Schneider
+Real-time LV visibility and power-quality data.
–Built for grid ops, not theft attribution; pricey, and doesn't separate heat-loss from theft.
Rules on SCADA

Fixed-threshold loss alarms

SCADA / spreadsheet loss reports
+Cheap, uses data you already have.
–Floods with false positives every hot day — so operators mute it.
Edge + AI · ours

Grid-Pulse NTL

transformer-level sensing + AI baseline
+Separates theft from natural loss, explains why, on the legacy grid — one node per feeder.
+Low CapEx, ROI < 2 months, no AMI required.

04

Where we're honest

No tool wins on every axis. Here's what the incumbents genuinely do better — and why we complement them rather than pretend to replace everything.

What AMI & incumbents do better

  • Per-customer billing data at the meter — we localize to the feeder/transformer, not the exact household.
  • Mature, standards-certified revenue metering and vendor support at national scale.
  • Rich historical datasets where AMI is already deployed (mostly US / EU).

How Grid-Pulse complements them

  • Runs today on grids that were never metered — no rollout to wait for.
  • One node covers a whole feeder of customers — a fraction of per-meter CapEx.
  • Narrows theft to a transformer + explains why, so inspectors and AMI both aim better.
  • Sits alongside AMI as the theft-attribution layer where meter data alone is noisy.
